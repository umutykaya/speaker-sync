    Calibrate by ear

    1. Place the speakers side by side, about 1 m away. Sit centered.
    2. Play the click. A double click means one speaker is early.
    3. Mute speakers one at a time to find the earliest; delay the others to match the latest one.
    4. Add delay to the early one with +10, then fine-tune with ±1 until you hear one click.
    5. Save a profile.
